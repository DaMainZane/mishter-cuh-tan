- **125** Tidebound tides are Event Deck events (`packs/tidebound/tides.ts` rewritten; the cluster schedule, spring tides and per-level period/strength are gone). The tide card (`tidebound.tide`, waves icon) picks a height (1: 70%, 2: 25%, 3: 5%) and 2 to 5 rounds from the world seed and its round (`tidePick`), so the timeline shows `Tide 2 · 3 rounds` before it fires. Every land tile joined to the coast through tiles no higher than the height floods at once (`floodThresholds`, `floodSet`; fog stops the water). A tide card while a tide is up raises it by 1 (at most 3) and keeps the longer time left (`stepTide`; the user's example, tide 1 with 2 left hit by a 3-round tide becomes tide 2 for 3 rounds, is a test). The tide retreats at the start of `untilRound` (`applyTides`, which also brings the board in line every round and clears only the floods it made). The timeline detail, the one-turn warning and the card share one projection (`projectTide`), which also follows a pending Fate veto. The Tide level slider now sets the number of tide cards (Very low 1 to Very high 5). Fish replenish reads the sea beside the land the tide flooded (depth 2 at height 2+). Bots: flood share = tide up-time from the deck mix x chance the height reaches the tile. Client: a "Tide 2, until R14" chip leads the timeline strip while a tide is up; the title card says the height, the rounds and whether it stacked. Old saves with the cluster schedule have their floods cleared. With the Event Deck off, Tides does nothing. **Map:** elevation 1 is now 50% of the land (was 65%, `ELEVATION_2_FROM` 0.5), 2 is 40%, 3 is 10%. Measured on default Tidebound maps before the change: height 1 covered about 65% of the land, height 2 90%, height 3 all of it. **Heat map:** orange (1), olive (2), green (3) instead of yellow to red (`--elev-N`, `terrainCanvas.ts` fallbacks; `tests/elevationtint.test.ts` rewritten for it). Old `tests/tideboundtides.test.ts` retired to `retired/[del.]tideboundtides-schedule.test.ts`, new one in its place (31); `tideboundbots`, `tideboundfishgrounds`, `tideboundrisingsea`, `tideboundmap`, `viewState` updated; `tests/view.snapshot.json` updated as a reviewed diff (Tidebound scenes now have the deck on: `eventTimeline`, tide `height` / `untilRound`, warning `height` / `rounds`, the elevation map, new flood tiles).
