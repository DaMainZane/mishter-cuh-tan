## The problem

On bigger radii the Tidebound map is a few big islands with one uniform, very wide sea gap, so the middle of the board is empty ocean and bridges have little use. Today Tidebound sets the map to the `islands` shape plus the `wide_water` perk (`TIDEBOUND_MAP_PICKS` in `src/server/lobby.ts`). `islands` makes 2 to 8 islands (`round(baseRadius / 1.5)`, min size 6), and `wide_water` with its default extra of 4 makes every gap 5 tiles (`wideGap` in `src/shared/mapshape.ts`) and grows the canvas by `ceil(4 * 1.5) = 6` rings of sea.

The user drew the target: many islands of mixed size, grouped in clusters, some only one or two bridges apart, with open sea still around them.

## Decided by the user (2026-11-24, their words in quotes where it matters)

1. Tidebound does not use a fixed preset shape: "as long as it ensures procedural generation of bigger radius maps with stacked island, that's good." My reading, which the user did not object to: a new procedural shape (working name `island_clusters`) that becomes Tidebound's default pick, so every Tidebound map is generated by it.
2. Gaps: **3 tiles minimum between clusters**. Inside a cluster islands can be 1 to 2 bridges apart. "If 2 clusters were to spawn on top of each other, merge them into 1. Randomize it, that gives strategical adaptation depth."
3. A cluster may be isolated; there is no need for every cluster to be reachable by short hops.
4. Island sizes 4 to 20 tiles, "more small than big, maybe 65-25-10": 65% small, 25% medium, 10% large. My split of the bands: small 4 to 7, medium 8 to 13, large 14 to 20.
5. No guaranteed start island per player (the lobby takes up to 8 players).
6. Keep the same land amount as now; the open sea moves to the edges and between clusters, not one big middle gap.

## Geometry facts to build on

- A bridge goes on an ocean edge (both sides water, `isOceanEdge` in `src/packs/tidebound/bridgeCore.ts`). Across a one-tile strait the edge between two neighbouring water tiles joins a coast corner of each island, so **a 1-tile gap is crossed by 1 bridge** and a 2-tile gap by 2 to 3. So "1 to 2 bridges apart" means a gap of 1 to 2 water tiles inside a cluster.
- Land count: `round(cellsWithin(baseRadius) * density / 100)` with the shape's `land` default (55 for `islands`), in `src/shared/mapshape.ts`. That is about 93 land tiles at radius 7, 397 at radius 15, 1535 at radius 30.
- With the 65/25/10 mix the mean island is about 7.9 tiles, so expect about **12 islands at radius 7, 50 at radius 15, 195 at radius 30**.
- The canvas is `baseRadius + 6` rings with `wide_water` at 4. At radius 30 the land is about 38% of that canvas, which is tight once every island needs a 1-tile moat and clusters need 3. **Check the packing before promising radius 30.** If it does not fit, the fallback is to grow the sea rings with the radius for this shape (not to drop land); tell the user which you chose.

## What to build

1. A new base op in `src/packs/base/map/shapes.ts` next to `islands` and `archipelago` (or in the Tidebound pack if it should only exist with the DLC on; base is fine since the shape is generic). Steps that work with the existing tools (`pickCenters`, `growBlobs`, `splitSizes`, `centersTooClose`):
   - Draw island sizes from the 65/25/10 bands until the land target is used up (seeded from the shape's stage RNG, never `g.rng`).
   - Group islands into clusters (random cluster sizes, roughly 2 to 6 islands).
   - Place cluster centres with at least a 3-tile gap between clusters' land; if two clusters would overlap, merge them into one cluster.
   - Inside a cluster, grow islands with a 1 or 2 tile gap (random per pair), never touching.
   - Merging must never fuse two islands into one bigger than 20 tiles: islands keep their own moat.
2. Make it Tidebound's default: change `TIDEBOUND_MAP_PICKS` in `src/server/lobby.ts` to the new shape plus `wide_water` (keep `wide_water`: it also writes land elevation and fish, `afterGenerated` in `src/packs/tidebound/bridges.ts`).
3. Leave `islands`, `archipelago` and every old share code generating exactly as before (`tests/mapgen.test.ts`, `tests/mapshapes.test.ts`, `tests/golden.snapshot.json` stay as they are).
4. Read `src/shared/mapcheck.ts` and the setup placement rules: anything that assumes an island of 7+ tiles (for example a table needing room) must still pass with many 4 to 7 tile islands, or the generator retries forever.
5. Bots: confirm the Tidebound bots still place settlements and bridges sensibly on a many-island map (`tests/tideboundbots.test.ts`, `tests/simmatrix.test.ts`).

## Acceptance (measure over many seeds, at radius 7, 15 and 30)

- Island size histogram matches 65/25/10 within a few points; no island under 4 or over 20.
- Minimum land-to-land gap between clusters is 3; inside clusters there are 1 and 2 tile gaps.
- A "largest empty sea" number (the biggest disc of water with no land) is clearly smaller than today's at radius 15 and 30.
- Generation stays inside `tests/mapbudget.test.ts` (radius 8 about 20 ms; check what it says for bigger radii).
- A full bot sim on the new map finishes (`tests/sim.test.ts` style).
- With Tidebound off nothing changes.
